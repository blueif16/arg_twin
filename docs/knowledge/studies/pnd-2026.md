# Privileged noise distillation: pose error displaces the action frame (Chang et al., 2026)

`[pnd-2026]` · status: verified · local: `data/refs/pnd-2026.pdf`

Read 2026-10-24 from arXiv 2610.07745v1 (Chang, Chuang, Lee, Chen, Yang, Sun; NTHU/NYCU). Every number is theirs. Lines starting "For us" are our reading. This is the source of the "31.5% vs 88.7%" claim.

## Setup

- **Tasks:** the three unmodified FORGE tasks in Isaac Lab: peg insertion with 0.114 mm radial clearance, gear meshing, and M16 nut threading.
- **Noise:** Gaussian fixture-pose noise σ. The same noisy estimate enters the observation and anchors the action frame, so before contact the offset cannot be identified from proprioception.
- **Student:** sees noisy state, a raw 6-axis wrench window and two RGB cameras. Trained with BC, then one DAgger round.
- **Teacher options:** an RL teacher that sees the true offset, or closed-form relabelling of clean demos into the displaced frame.

## Results

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Official FORGE policy, true pose → σ = 5 mm | 97–99% → 31.8 / 49.9 / 60.4% (peg/gear/nut) |
| Student (teacher route), σ = 0–5 mm | 92–99%; six non-privileged baselines 2–80% |
| Matched BC on peg, σ = 5 mm: demos without offset / privileged / relabelled | 31.5 / 88.7 / 95.8% |
| Failures of the non-privileged student | 87–100% are contact without insertion |
| Real Franka, zero-shot, pooled n = 90 per level, σ = 0/1/2.5/5 mm | student 92.2/91.1/85.6/83.3% vs T-A+ 88.9/85.6/68.9/34.4% |
| Real, 2.5 mm gap | 17 points, Fisher exact p = 0.012 |
| Real, 5 mm gap | 49 points, Wilson [74, 90] vs [25, 45] |

- **Sensitivity on hardware:**
  - no photometric augmentation: 6–50% instead of 84–97% at 5 mm;
  - light instead of dark table: peg 93% → 25%;
  - camera extrinsic error 10 mm/2° instead of 5 mm/1°: gear success roughly halves.
- **Beyond the training range:** peg 94.5 / 88.8 / 74.2 / 55.5% at σ = 7.5 / 10 / 15 / 20 mm.
- **Unseen geometries:** on 70 AutoMate plug–socket pairs at σ = 2.5 mm (n = 32 per cell) the student succeeds in only 4.6% of trials, against 18.4% for the state baseline. Robustness to pose error does not carry over to new shapes.

## Limits

- The main results are simulation. The hardware sweep has 30 trials per cell. Several arms use one seed.
- Pose noise is injected artificially into the taught fixture pose; the physical parts never move.

## For us

- For us: a frame error of a few millimetres turns contact into jams at sub-0.2 mm clearance. That is the same failure signature we must reproduce: contact without insertion. Board and TCP calibration (B0) therefore matter more than contact parameters for `B2.outcome_bacc`.
- For us: deployable sensing alone did not fix the offset. Supervision that knows the offset did. When Argon practises under a randomized board offset, success checks and teacher signals may use the true offset in sim, but the policy or skill sees only the noisy one. That is this paper's design.
- For us: relabelling clean trajectories into a displaced frame in closed form is cheap and needs no re-rendering. It is a way to turn REASSEMBLE successes into offset-robust demonstrations.
