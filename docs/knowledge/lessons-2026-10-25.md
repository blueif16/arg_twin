# Lessons, 2026-10-25: what today's Argon runs taught

This is a digest of the day's best-evidenced lessons. Each one names its evidence path; read that file before acting on a number. "Argon" means `gemini-4-argon` measured live, and "stand-in" means a Claude subagent. The two are never merged. These are local findings: published best practice is in the topic files of this folder, and their sources are in `references.md`.

## 1. API settings (evidence: `runs/argon/probes/api/findings.md`)

- **Medium thinking is adaptive and often skips thinking.** Low never thinks. High always thinks, at 3–7× the latency. Thought tokens show as 0 whenever it skipped.
- **Never ask for computed numbers as bare JSON at medium.**
  - Medium with JSON `response_format` gave confident wrong numbers (1/3 right, one 186 px off).
  - Medium plus `code_execution` got 6/6 exact in about 19 s, for $0.018 per problem.
  - High with JSON got 3/3, but took 87 s.
- **Video `fps` inside a `function_result` is silently ignored.** Send clips in a user step. For moments, use a 2 fps time-only frame grid (MAE 0.35 s) or 4 fps video. The 1 fps default snaps every moment to a second (bias −0.58 s).
- **Implicit caching pays only along a chain.**
  - Stateless calls shared no cache unless byte-identical.
  - Chained practice turns cached 72–88%.
  - On LIBERO, the published stateless protocol cached only 2.6%: image eviction rewrites history every call (`runs/libero/feas-argon-high-1/report.md`).
- **Two settings misbehave without failing.**
  - `tool_choice: "none"` strips the tool declarations from the prompt.
  - Agentic video works only as `"processing": "agentic"` (a string).
- **Long or high calls hang synchronously.**
  - Use a 300 s client timeout with retries, then resume the chain from the last good turn (`runs/argon/probes/practice/findings.md`).
  - Streaming survived a 10 min high call that timed out sync at 25 min (`runs/argon/probes/scene/findings.md`).
  - A chain cannot continue from an `incomplete` interaction.
- **Settings per job:**

  | Job | Thinking | Other settings |
  |---|---|---|
  | Read, practice and code | medium | chained practice, images at medium resolution |
  | One-off design or calibration math with no tools | high | |

- **Budget:**

  | Job | Cost |
  |---|---|
  | Reading | $0.03–0.05 per recording minute |
  | Practice | $0.01–0.02 per turn |
  | Generated code | $0.15 per 1k lines |

## 2. Long-video strategy (evidence: `runs/argon/probes/longvideo/findings.md`)

- **Robot logs with signals: signals first.**
  - Signals + video scored F1 0.93 with a 0.23 s boundary error, at $0.041 per recording minute.
  - Video added nothing measurable over signals only.
  - The deterministic draft alone scored F1 0.92.
  - On the stage-A gate, the Argon read scored segment F1 0.992 and failure bacc 0.947 ($11.53 for 9 reads; `runs/argon/run-argon-A-1/`).
- **Video only: one 1 fps whole-video overview, then zooms.**

  | Strategy | F1 | Boundary error | Cost per minute |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Overview only | 0.80 | 1.6 s | $0.014 |
  | Overview + grid zooms | 0.84 | 1.1 s | $0.026 |
  | Overview + 4–5 fps video zooms in a user step | 0.85 | 0.92 s | $0.068 |

  Zooming found zero extra failures. Cap it at 2 levels and 30% of the recording.
- **Transfer to human assembly video is weak.** Assembly101 scored F1 0.45–0.49, because of dataset conventions rather than perception. Write the step definitions into the prompt.

## 3. Calibration recipe (evidence: `runs/argon/probes/b0/findings.md`)

- **Native one-look 3D guesses are 0.27–0.66 m off.** Distance is underestimated by about 35%. Never use them as the answer.
- **Ask for pixel picks as normalized `[y, x]` 0–1000.** That form was clean in 4/4 runs. Picks asked as (u, v) drifted into the 0–1000 frame in 6 of 10 runs, giving 180–400 px errors.
- **The recipe that worked:**
  1. Pick the tool-centre point on about 8 frames.
  2. Pair each pick with the logged position (`real_frame`).
  3. Fit with `refine_camera` at a fixed focal length: a 3.1–3.2 px seed.
  4. Run one `calibrate_static_cameras` call: 1.43 / 1.72 px held-out, 0.77 px two-view, $0.32.
- **Known traps:**
  - Episode-0 numbers logged as "native" priors.
  - Hand-tuning overlays instead of reseeding: 52 `render_overlay` calls ended at 4.46 px.
  - `save_world_frame` silently writing cameras at position 0.
- **High thinking with 6 images never answered.** Sync calls timed out at 20 and 60 min, and background mode rejects image input.

## 4. Scene-as-code limits (evidence: `runs/argon/probes/scene/findings.md`)

- **Argon writes physically sound MJCF:** 10 of 12 files passed load, penetration, settle and dt/2 checks.
- **It cannot place the board from images.** The best hole xy was 7 mm median against the hand-built twin's 1.2 mm, and the plate top was 3–24 mm too low.
- **A handedness prior overrides what it sees.** 3 of 7 chains flipped a correctly read mirror to det +1 at least once, putting holes 150–300 mm off.
- **It anchors on the mocap pose** (9–10 mm off). Depth along the viewing direction is ambiguous with both cameras looking from +x.
- **Revisions can make things worse, and Argon cannot tell:** its only error signal is overlays it over-reads, and it claimed checks that never ran.
- **Lesson:** Argon writes the scene; contact and calibration tools place it.

## 5. Compile (evidence: `runs/argon/probes/compile/findings.md`)

- **Argon wrote and verified a sandboxed insertion routine on its own:** 879 lines, 8 iterations on 6 dev starts, 0 flags in the end.
- **On the 24 pilot starts with zero brain calls:**

  | Condition | Argon's routine | Claude's v1 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Routine alone | 8/24 | 5/24 |
  | True hole given | 9/24 | 16/24 |

- **Misses are perception and grip:** 10 NOT_FOUND (the hole lies outside the 7 mm spiral) and 6 JAMMED. Keep hole-finding with the brain; the routine owns the motion.
- **Practice reports leak true geometry.** Argon used the env's final-offset report as an oracle ("trilateration"). Judge only on starts the compiler never ran, and pick dev starts that span the error range.
- **Cost:** about $5 and 1 h per compile attempt at high thinking. Use background mode and retry connection errors while polling.

## 6. Footage → reward (evidence: `runs/argon/probes/reward/findings.md`, `runs/argon/peg_reward/results/eval_v0_partial.json`)

- **One demo clip plus a state API document is enough** for Argon to write `success` and `progress`. Medium, write plus one verification turn, costs about $0.08 and 65 s per task.
- **First writes from pixels are loose, never strict.** Drawer: −0.11 m against −0.14 m. Peg: depth 1.5–3 mm short and a 4° tilt accepted, for a near-miss rejection of 0.625 on 392 near-misses (`runs/argon/peg_reward/results/gate_v0.summary.txt`). Recall stays near 1.0.
- **A revision can over-correct.** The peg's v1 closed every near-miss (1.000) but recall fell to 0.88: release was over-tightened, and 2 successes stall at progress 0.95 (`gate_v1.summary.txt`). Gate on precision, recall and near-misses together, and re-run the whole gate after every revision.
- **The verification turn fixes the threshold exactly when it shows the first disagreeing state.** It does nothing when the feedback demos hold no errors, and a revision can chase a looser benchmark predicate.
- **Benchmark predicates can be looser than intent.** LIBERO accepts a mug tilted 75° (12/20) and butter hovering in the basket (21/21). Score rewards against both the predicate and intent near-misses.
- **The gate predicts practice.** The mug reward, which fails the gate (P 0.928, near-miss rejection 0.865), produced the chain's first reward-only success (`runs/argon/libero_chain/runs/lc-mug-i27/results.json`).
- Skill: `playbook/skills/reward-from-video/`.

## 7. Integrity (evidence: `runs/claude/run-standin-1/replay/audit_v6/summary.json`; progress log 2026-10-25)

- **The B2 registration leak.**
  - Every v3–v6 per-attempt registration read the release frame, which comes after first contact in 99.3% of attempts.
  - Registering before contact only dropped the twin's seat rate from 0.692 to 0.588.
  - Permuting the per-attempt board shift dropped it to 0.391, so the shift encoded where each part ended up.
  - A second channel: the board fit used success-labelled seat events, including the attempt's own.
  - The leak inflated the seat rate by about 0.10; it did not inflate per-attempt discrimination.
- **Why it matters:** a leak can hide inside a fitted parameter. Audit with a known-leak control (permutation, before-contact only), not only by reading code.
- **Observers never pass sim truth** (rules.md from v17).
  - Every nudge, reflection or audit input is built from what the actor received, plus outcome reports the split allows and the frozen reward's outputs.
  - Both observer designs follow it (`harness/brains/trials.py`, `runs/argon/libero_chain/chain.py`), and `analyze.py` `leak_grep` checks it (`L.observer_leaks`). At 15:40: 0 hits in 24 observer texts.
- **Exam reads are counted per brain.** Episodes leaked through listings are burned (`playbook/exam_burned.json`). Frozen runs are re-verified by hash before anything that could change them (`practice_manifest {verify: true}`).

## 8. The pilot and the auditor's verdict (evidence: `runs/orchestrator/pilot-loop-1/audit/final_1440.md`, `runs/argon/probes/audit/findings.md`, `runs/argon/pilot/results.md`)

- **Stand-in pilot, 24 paired starts.** Arm 1 (raw poses + images) beat arm N (task tools + routine v1): 21/24 vs 16/24 counted, 5–0 discordant (p = 0.0625). The auditor's call was **iterate**:
  - measure the tip before the routine (a skill step);
  - routine v2 gets tilt and yaw tunables, a chamfer yaw test and release on jam;
  - an env force reflex;
  - one scratch dir per run.
- **What wins is what gets measured:**
  - Hole minus tip in one image with the tip on the plate gave 17/24 first-descent seats.
  - The arm that read only the hole sank its first probe on 1/24.
  - `force_max` did not bound force on wedged parts (14.6–74.3 N, 2 seats voided).
- **Argon pilot, interim at 15:33** (attempt 1, raw poses, medium): 11/13 counted (CI 0.55–0.98), 2 stalls on square pegs. The same-start trials with reflection (`runs/argon/trials/`) run on its failures.
- **Argon as trial auditor.**
  - The majority of 3 medium 4 fps calls caught 4/4 planted cheats, with 0 false alarms and 14/16 outcomes right, at about $0.17 per trial.
  - Text only caught 1/4; high thinking raised 2/4 false alarms.
  - It is a second line behind the monitors, which caught all 4 themselves. The cheats were blatant.
- Skill: `playbook/skills/auditor/`.

## 9. LIBERO anchor (evidence: `runs/libero/feas-argon-high-1/report.md`, `playgrounds/libero/README.md`)

- **The published protocol reproduces** cost and calls: $5.70 vs $5.90 per episode on the same tasks, 45 vs 50 calls. Success was 2/6 (CI 0.04–0.78).
- **Protocol defects found:**
  - The published agentview is mirrored left-right, which compromises the two left/right tasks (the paper's 0/180 tasks).
  - The tool text says rotations are relative, but the adapter takes them as absolute.
  - A Gemini input filter blocked a benign transcript.
- **Half of the failures were false `done` claims.** Self-verification is the gap, as in the paper (287 of 1,800).
