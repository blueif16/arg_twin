- Why `new_calib`: its README says each joint's zero is the middle of its joint range, which is the zero of LeRobot's
  normalised units (RANGE_M100_100: 0 = middle of the recorded range). The mapping from dataset units to these
  joint angles is still fitted per joint from video (twin/so101_robot.py), because the dataset carries no
  calibration file.
- Upstream notes kept in mind: generated by onshape-to-robot; base collision meshes removed; the gripper joint is a
  hinge (`gripper`, -10..100 deg), while LeRobot reports it as 0 (closed) .. 100 (open).
